Core features

The platform layers built around student outcomes.

Syllabus + progress tracking

  • Course selection with subject and unit visibility
  • Progress state tracking per topic and subject
  • Continue Learning based on the next incomplete topic
  • Restart Course confirms before resetting all progress

Study tools

  • AI notes generation
  • Personal notes and revision notes
  • Study planner and daily scheduling
  • Bookmarking important resources and questions

Assessment and improvement

  • Quiz generator with scoring and explanations
  • Mock tests with timers and weak-topic reporting
  • Performance analysis using local demo data
  • Topic performance summaries

Career and communication

  • Mock interviews for technical and HR preparation
  • Project and skill resources
  • Notifications and announcements
  • Support tickets and complaint management
