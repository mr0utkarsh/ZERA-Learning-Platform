FAQ

Questions students often ask.

Is ZERA free?

Yes. ZERA is designed as a free student learning platform with accessible demo functionality and a future-ready architecture.

Does this frontend use a real AI connection?

No. AI tools run in Demo Mode until a real provider and backend are connected.

Can progress be saved locally?

Yes. Progress, notes, study plans, and bookmarks are stored in localStorage for safe browser-based demo use.
